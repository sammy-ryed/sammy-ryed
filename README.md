## 5 things I built that I'm not embarrassed about

&nbsp;

**MusAIc** — [repo](https://github.com/sammy-ryed/MusAIc---Feel-the-Music-of-Your-Mood)

your face is sad. your words are sadder. so I made an AI that reads both and plays you the exact Spotify song you need. you're welcome / I'm sorry.

> `Python` `FastAPI` `OpenCV` `HuggingFace` `Spotify API`

&nbsp;

**Bunker** — [repo](https://github.com/sammy-ryed/Bunker) · [live](https://bunker-five.vercel.app/)

apocalypse. one spot in the bunker. you decide who survives using **priority queues**.
yes this is a fullstack web app. yes it's about deciding which of your classmates deserves to live.

> `Next.js` `FastAPI` `MySQL` `Vercel` `Railway`

&nbsp;

**BurstTime Chronicles** — [repo](https://github.com/sammy-ryed/BurstTime-Chronicles)

I made a CPU scheduling simulator and gave it a lore. processes *arrive*. they *wait*. they *suffer*.
sometimes they get CPU time. visualises FCFS, SJF, Round Robin with a slick UI.

> `Python` `Tkinter` `sv-ttk`

&nbsp;

**Bhandi** — [repo](https://github.com/sammy-ryed/Bhandi) · [play it and lose 2 hours](https://bhandi.netlify.app/)

flappy bird. in a browser. from scratch. bhandi means "that container you throw all your random stuff into."
that's the entire explanation. custom physics. power-ups. trippy music. dignity not included.

> `JavaScript` `HTML` `CSS`

&nbsp;

**FlatMate** — *(private, for now)*

hinge but for finding a roommate. built for **SRM KTR students**.
match on personality, sleep schedule, cleanliness standards — the stuff that actually matters.
fullstack with real auth and a real database. phase 1 done. this one's going somewhere.

> `Next.js` `TypeScript` `MySQL` `NextAuth.js` `Tailwind CSS`
